# Visual direction

User-selected direction: “Light editorial: warm paper, dark ink, Spain red, clear pitch diagrams”. Apply antislop during the build.

Audience: football analysts investigating pressure sequences from Euro 2024 event data.

The page uses warm paper for extended reading and dark ink for labels. Spain red identifies pressure; green identifies established possession. Serif section accents give the requested editorial character; system sans-serif keeps dense controls and tables legible. Pitch lines and spatial grids carry actual football information. Replay motion is limited to the ball's event-level interpolation, with manual scrubbing available. No decorative animation or generated imagery.

Energy 2 / Rhythm 2 / Motion 1. The primary visual relationship is between pressure location and the recorded regain; sequence inspection follows directly below. Comparisons use the same recovery definition and explicit denominators.
