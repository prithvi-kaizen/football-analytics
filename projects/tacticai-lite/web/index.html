Dots show observed rates; lines show descriptive 95% Wilson intervals. The intervals assume independent corners and do not account for dependence within matches. These associations do not establish that changing delivery causes more shots.

What this system can support

Explicit definitions

Short means a recorded pass length of at most 15 standardized units. Swing uses the provider’s recorded technique. Unknown techniques remain unknown. Multiple shots count once for the binary outcome.

360 measures visible players, box counts, centroids, spread, nearest-defender distances and density within the camera-visible part of the box. Missing frames and unseen players are not zero defensive density.

Inspect, then interpret

This public site contains aggregate findings. The Python/Streamlit project adds local corner inspection, linked player frames, event timelines and configuration search after downloading directly from the provider.

360 is broadcast snapshots, not continuous tracking. It cannot establish runs, marking assignments or tactical intent. This is inspired by TacticAI, not a reproduction of its proprietary data or model.
