# Design direction

User chose a light analysis board and antislop during implementation.
Reading this as: a corner analysis workbench for coaches and technical readers, in a light analysis-board style, dial ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

The primary decision is which observed corner to inspect and whether a pattern merits further review. A pitch with the actual linked frame is the focal point. Use native Streamlit controls above the evidence, no invented logo or marketing imagery. Show the product name as text. StatsBomb's official logo is required source attribution.

Use warm white paper, charcoal text, dark green attackers and ochre outlined defenders with distinct marker shapes. A restrained green accent connects the controls to the pitch. Standard sans-serif text supports dense match/event reading; no external font dependency. Charts and event tables sit in their analytical context; no decorative cards, gradients, shadows, motion or badges. Stack columns on narrow screens; preserve native focus and add a high-contrast focus ring. No automatic network downloads from the UI.
