When a defensive line steps up, is the deepest defender actually being left behind?

I looked at two matches of public SkillCorner tracking for One Step Behind.

One Adelaide sequence reaches a 3.53 m depth gap. But the deepest defender has moved 1.76 m forward while his teammates' median advance is 2.36 m. Being deeper and remaining behind are different things.

The frozen method found 0 qualifying candidates in 44 held-out steps. A simple instantaneous-gap baseline flagged two. I kept the zero result, along with the missingness audit and threshold sensitivity.

The replay shows real coordinates, observation flags and an adjustable tolerance. No defensive-error claims.
