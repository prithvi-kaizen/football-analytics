When a defensive line steps up, does every defender move with it?

I built One Step Behind to explore defensive-line movement using SkillCorner’s open tracking data. The interactive pitch lets you replay real sequences, inspect depth gaps and adjust the positional tolerance.

Across 93 eligible steps in two matches, none met the full lagging-defender rule. One defender reached a 3.53 m gap while still moving forward with his teammates.

⛳️ My main learnings:
➤ Being deeper doesn’t automatically mean a defender failed to step up.
➤ Missing and estimated positions limit which sequences can be assessed.
➤ Gap size alone isn’t enough. Movement over time and threshold choices matter.

Built with Python, NumPy and JavaScript, with the browser replay deployed on Vercel.
