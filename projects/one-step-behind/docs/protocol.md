# Feasibility and frozen protocol

Protocol recorded on 9 November 2026, after schema/coverage audit and before detector implementation. The configuration file is hashed into results. No parameters are fitted to held-out events.

## Evidence and scope

[SkillCorner Open Data](https://github.com/SkillCorner/opendata) revision `4340d274572876239c154c90bc507a9b3250a656` supplies 20 catalogue entries and tracking LFS assets despite README wording of ten. This is not a completeness audit of all 20. Saved upstream catalogue, tree, pointer files and MIT notice are in `source/`. Two cached matches pass all six upstream hash checks. The cache saves download time; sampling is by availability, not random. Brisbane Roar v Perth Glory (1925299, 21 December 2024) is development; Sydney FC v Adelaide United (1996435, 1 February 2025) is an algorithm holdout, not an unseen dataset to the author, who used it in Marking Handoffs.

JSONL has frame, timestamp, period, ball_data, possession, image_corners_projection and player_data. Player records contain player_id, x, y, is_detected. Coordinates are metres, centre origin, length on x, both pitches 105 x 68 m. Metadata contains player roles, team IDs, shirt numbers, playing intervals and half directions. CSV phase intervals are half-open, in-play context; they are inferred labels, not independent tactical annotation. No line, stepping, marking or error ground truth exists.

Coverage audit: development has 61,301 frames and 1,054,724 player rows, with 57.89% of valid positions detected. Holdout has 57,621 frames and 957,682 player rows, 65.80% detected. Respectively 13,340 and 14,071 period frames contain no player positions. There are 151/20 out-of-bounds rows (5 m margin), 0/3 implausible speed steps (>12.5 m/s), 85/72 phase-period conflicts and 5,080/5,000 known phase/tracking possession conflicts. These are audited, not silently interpolated. Both speed-step endpoints are rejected.

Decision: feasible only as a role-backed, geometry-filtered pilot. Full-match coverage is insufficient for universal defensive-line or tactical-error claims. Complete team geometry can include many extrapolated players; the observed-only sensitivity must report its own denominator.

## Related approaches and contribution

1. [Carrilho et al., Sensors 20(17), 4990 (2020)](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/20/17/4990), *Using Optical Tracking System Data to Measure Team Synergic Behavior*. Cluster phase analysis of player-ball-goal angles studies player-team synchrony. This motivates temporal, relational measurement; it does not supply a metre threshold for a lagging defender. We use direct depth and displacement instead of oscillatory phase estimates on short clips.
2. [The tactics of successful attacks, Journal of Sports Sciences (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2020.1834689) identifies dynamic subgroups through formation descriptors and clustering, then compares subgroup centroids and synchrony. It supports examining units rather than whole-team centroids. Here metadata roles constrain membership and ambiguous shapes are excluded; we do not claim to reproduce its learned subgroup method.
3. [Bauer, Anzer & Shaw (2023), Putting team formations in association football into context](https://doi.org/10.3233/JSA-220620) studies phase-specific formation differences and geometric relative positions. It motivates separating out-of-possession context. We use provider phases and agreeing possession, not a trained phase model.

Marking Handoffs models changes in attacker-defender coverage. Rest Defence Analyzer measures transition protection before losing possession using DFL data. One Step Behind measures within-line longitudinal separation during collective forward motion using SkillCorner data. Neither defensive mistakes nor intended offside traps can be inferred.

## Frozen operational definitions

Normalize x by the defending team's attack direction, so forward is increasing x and deeper is smaller x. Rotate both axes for display. Require valid ball, known agreeing tracking/phase possession, period consistency and build_up/create/finish phase. Require all ten outfield defensive positions. Metadata Central Defender and Full Back roles define a possible line of 3, 4 or 5 active players (at least two central defenders). All role defenders must be exactly the deepest N outfield players, span at least 12 m laterally and no more than 12 m in depth. This excludes midfield cover, advanced fullbacks and unclear units; it does not force a back four. Membership, period, possession and phase ID must remain fixed. Any missing/invalid position breaks continuity. Playing intervals use source half-specific frame intervals.

At 10 Hz, identify endpoints where at least N-1 defenders advance >=1.5 m over the preceding second and the same N-1 advance >=0.5 m in each half-second. Initial depth spread must be <=3 m. Consecutive qualifying endpoints form one stepping episode; overlapping one-second movement windows are merged. Episode duration covers the movement interval, not just qualifying endpoints. Exclude movement windows crossing an eligibility break.

For each defender i, reference depth is median(x of other line players); gap_i = reference - x_i. The displayed common line is the all-player median; dispersion is max(x)-min(x). A one-defender candidate requires gap >=3 m, other defenders' spread <=3 m, gap growth >=1.5 m since episode anchor, and defender advancement <= half the median peer advancement from that anchor. Require the same identity to meet the rules for >=0.5 s inside the movement episode or its next second. Only the earliest candidate is counted per stepping episode; ties use greater gap then player ID. These thresholds are transparent geometric choices, not estimated tactical tolerances or validated error cutoffs.

Follow that identity for up to 10 s from candidate onset within the same eligible context. Recovery requires all line-player depths within 3 m for five consecutive bins. Report elapsed time to the first of those bins; observation lasts through confirmation. Gap duration is the first uninterrupted run with that defender's gap >=3 m. A gap ending without observation is censored. Recovery is censored at missing geometry, context/line change or the cap. Never treat censoring as successful recovery. Report observed recovery distribution separately; no population median or accuracy without ground truth. Recovery may occur through peers dropping back, not necessarily the lagging player stepping up.

## Evaluation planned before results

Report eligible team-seconds, line sizes, stepping episodes and their duration, frame-level gap/spread quantiles, candidate steps / all steps, gap and recovery duration distributions and censoring. Baseline: an instantaneous gap >=3 m at any frame in the same step/search window, without persistence, peer-shape, growth or lag-motion requirements. This is a retrieval comparison, not accuracy. Sensitivity reruns gap 2/3/4 m and persistence 0.3/0.5/0.8 s; eligibility is fixed. Observed-only rerun requires every defensive outfielder detected, an intentionally strict lower-coverage population, plus fixed-population support for candidate windows. Select up to three chronological holdout candidates, prioritizing entirely detected line windows before extrapolated ones; if fewer exist, say so. No threshold optimization for attractive clips.
