What this can establish

The detector retrieves changes between plausible covering defenders. A gap means no defender meets the configured geometry for at least 0.4 seconds.

Continuous cover is a candidate for review, not a verified successful handoff. A gap does not prove a defensive mistake, a missed instruction or a communication failure.

Extrapolated positions can create or hide gaps. The detected-trio filter concerns only the attacker and the two named defenders during the transfer window; it does not establish that every defender was observed.

The synthetic test uses known geometric changes. Real-data tactical precision and recall remain unknown without independent annotation.
