# Marking Handoffs viewer

Owner direction: "Analyst workbench: light pitch, compact controls, clear labels."

Reading this as an analyst workbench for football researchers, using a light tactical-board visual language. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. User requested antislop during construction. No skill installation or repository routing changes are needed.

- The pitch is the main focal point because the question concerns spatial responsibility changing over time.
- A warm off-white workspace and pale pitch reduce visual fatigue when inspecting many clips; dark mode remains available.
- Attacking blue and defending slate separate teams. A green incoming marker and amber outgoing outline encode the transfer, repeated in the legend and assignment timeline.
- Circles, explicit A / OUT / IN labels and solid/dashed outlines supplement color; hollow player markers disclose extrapolation.
- A Georgia title gives the project an editorial voice; system sans-serif makes dense evidence and controls readable without network fonts.
- A compact filter strip precedes a wide replay and a narrow evidence panel. On tablets the evidence moves below the pitch; on phones controls and filters stack.
- Space separates selection, replay and evidence. No decorative cards, promotional navigation, icons or invented logos are needed.
- Playback is deliberate and user-initiated. Frame stepping, a scrubber and a baseline toggle expose the mechanism. No automatic looping or decorative motion.
- The same replay data drives the self-contained HTML and the tracking-only video; the video makes no coaching-error claim.
