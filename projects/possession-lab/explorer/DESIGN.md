# Design direction

Research instrument for football analysts and ML engineers. Warm paper, editorial
serif titles and quiet system sans controls. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
The measured pitch is the focal point; movement occurs only during user playback.
Blue and orange identify teams consistently across pitch, labels and timeline.
Neutral grey denotes dead ball; outlined white denotes unknown. Labels accompany
colour throughout. Dark green locates the tracking in its football context.
The wide pitch plus narrow inspector lets a visitor compare space and inference.
Horizontal timeline rows align every model to the same frame. Tables carry actual
scores, not decorative statistic cards. Generous section spacing separates the
three research questions. No stock imagery, fabricated brand assets or animations.
Light and dark themes preserve the same hierarchy. Controls use native semantics.
