What this model assumes

A plausible target is an outfield teammate 5–40 m from the ball, on the pitch and behind a snapshot offside screen. The geometric baseline asks whether a defender lies within 1 m of the pass route, excluding 2 m at either end.

The arrival model samples the same route at no more than 0.5 m spacing. A 15 m/s ball competes with defenders using a 0.7 s reaction interval, capped past velocity and 5 m/s movement. A smooth function maps the best arrival margin to an obstruction index. No probability calibration, body orientation, pass technique or receiver movement is modelled.

SkillCorner Passing Option Ease and pressure categories are possession-level, proprietary outputs derived from shared tracking. The comparison evaluates agreement between proxies. No unattempted-pass outcomes, causal credit, action-free defensive value or player ranking are validated.
