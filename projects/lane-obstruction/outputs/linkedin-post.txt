How can we measure a defender’s contribution without counting tackles or interceptions?

I built The Defender Nobody Notices to explore passing-lane obstruction using SkillCorner’s open tracking and event data.

It finds possible passing targets, compares lane geometry with defender arrival times and lets you replay each situation on an interactive pitch.

Across 832 labelled possession starts in two held-out matches, simple geometry agreed more closely with SkillCorner’s passing-difficulty categories than the arrival-time model.

⛳️ My main learnings:
➤ A more complex model isn’t automatically a better one.
➤ Estimated off-camera positions matter when interpreting defensive positioning.
➤ Agreement with provider categories isn’t independent validation. An obstructed lane doesn’t prove a pass was prevented.

Built with Python, NumPy and pandas, with a browser version deployed on Vercel.
