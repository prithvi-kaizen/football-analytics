# Rest Defence Analyzer

Owner-selected direction: light analyst workspace, pitch-first views, restrained red and teal marks. Audience: football analysts investigating rest defence on historical open tracking data.

The match and turnover selector lead directly into a large pitch snapshot. Below it, the transition timeline and diagnostic scatterplot provide evidence. Metric definitions and original source records remain available in the same workspace.

Warm off-white background, charcoal text, red losing-team circles and teal counterattacking triangles. Shirt numbers and distinct shapes preserve identification without relying on colour. System sans-serif typography keeps dense labels legible. No photographic or decorative assets are needed.

Energy 2: the full pitch and contrasting team marks provide emphasis. Rhythm 2: a broad pitch view alternates with a compact numerical readout and full-width outcome chart. Motion 1: an explicit replay scrubber changes recorded frames; no ambient animation or interpolated movement.

Visual purpose: the pitch dominates because the analytical question concerns player structure. A single fixed light theme supports chart and pitch legibility. Red/teal encode roles, never good/bad judgements. Scores are plain numbers with explicit diagnostic labels.
