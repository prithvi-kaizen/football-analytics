What these measurements mean

A rule-based baseline

Each profile finds contiguous attacking movement at its speed threshold, lasting at least 1 s, travelling at least 5 m and progressing at least 3 m toward goal. Team possession comes from in-play phases; the runner stays at least 3 m from the tracked ball and outside their provider possession intervals.

Seven-frame smoothing reduces positional noise. Gaps, implausible raw jumps and source boundaries break trajectories. Missing carrier identities and extrapolated ball positions limit touch-free classification.

Space, defined precisely

A teammate is chosen from the starting state, before measuring change. Free patch area counts 1 m² cell centres within 6 m of that starting position, at least 3 m from every opponent. Context metrics require a complete, continuous opponent roster at both endpoints.

Behind-line runs cross the second-deepest outfield opponent. Wide runs finish in an outer channel while moving outward. Toward-space runs gain at least 2 m of nearest-opponent clearance. Categories can overlap.

Fully detected runners can still depend on extrapolated defenders. Strict coverage refers to the runner throughout the run, and contextual players and ball at both endpoints. It does not certify visibility between endpoints. No causal model or human-labelled accuracy study is included.
