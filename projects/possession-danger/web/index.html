What the metrics measure

Analytical possession
Provider possession ID, bounded by period and split when the other team passes, carries, shoots, dribbles, miscontrols, is dispossessed or shields. The provider's possession_team names the chain starter; defensive pressures and tackles do not switch the analytical owner.
Elapsed and active duration
Elapsed span includes gaps between owner actions. Active duration unions recorded owner on-ball action intervals and counts overlaps once. Both stop at the next analytical sequence. Neither is a continuous ball-in-play clock.
Territory
Field tilt is each team's share of completed passes starting in the final third (x ≥ 80 on a 120 × 80 pitch). Progression is last controlled x minus first x, in coordinate units, not metres.
Expected threat
The 12 × 8 grid solves V = r + TV using training matches only. Reward is empirical non-penalty goals per eligible action. Completed pass/carry transitions are divided by all eligible actions, including failures. Failed moves and explicit losses absorb at zero; every shot terminates the model path. Unseen cells remain zero.
Move values
Signed xT is V(end) − V(start) on successful passes/carries. Positive xT sums increases only and can count repeated advances. It is not a calibrated possession-goal probability. Penalties are excluded from xT but remain in observed shot/xG totals.
Score state and validation
The score is reconstructed before each indexed action, with own goals counted once and shootouts excluded. All final scores, passes, shots and shot xG reconcile with source data. Match-separated holdout prevents event-level leakage. Shot-choice Brier score checks one-step shot choice, not full xT calibration.
