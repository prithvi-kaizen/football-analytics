# Open Body viewer direction

Approved by the project owner on 28 October 2026: compact analyst workbench, off-white page, green tactical pitch, orange receiver/orientation marks, side-by-side 2D pitch and rotatable 3D skeleton.

Audience: football analysts and technically curious readers assessing whether shoulder orientation adds useful information at reception.

ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2. Motion is limited to user-controlled playback of real football frames and camera rotation. The viewer opens paused. A single light theme keeps comparisons legible. System sans-serif text supports dense analytical reading without font dependencies.

The pitch is the focal point. Orange identifies only the selected receiver and their shoulder heading. Other players use contrasting blue and white marks with team abbreviations. Off-camera estimates use hollow circles. Missing skeletons are shown as missing, never filled from future frames. Labels and text accompany color encodings.

The 3D skeleton uses player-centred XY and relative Z; no absolute ground-height claim. A labelled yaw slider supports keyboard camera rotation. A labelled frame slider and play/pause control support scrubbing. Results use a compact comparison with exact numbers and a labelled paired uncertainty interval. The two-match limit remains visible.

No logos, fictional avatars, marketing sections, decorative statistics or unmeasured tactical claims are needed. All numbers bind to experiment output.
