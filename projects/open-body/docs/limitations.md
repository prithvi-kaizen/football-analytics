# Limitations

* **Two games, four teams, one competition.** There are only two match environments. Neither a negative nor a positive held-out result demonstrates generalisation across football. The reverse split reuses these environments.
* **Observational associations.** Location, skill, footedness, tactical role, receiving pressure, incoming ball trajectory, match state and pass options jointly affect orientation and outcomes. The simple linear baseline does not adjust for all of them. Extra predictive information is not a causal intervention effect.
* **Selected pose availability.** Broadcast visibility, occlusion and posture influence pose availability. Conditioning on pose can change outcome prevalence and pressure/location distributions. Same-sample ablation reduces an unfair model comparison, but does not remove selection bias.
* **Shoulder proxy.** No independent heading ground truth is supplied. Joint error estimates are not calibrated angular intervals. Gating, smoothing and noise stress tests do not prove the heading correct. Left/right swaps and systematic errors remain. Shoulders do not identify gaze, scanning, intention or a “half-turn” technique.
* **Timing and interpolation.** Contact times and outcomes come from 10 Hz vendor events. Pose XY is interpolated to 25 Hz; this adds no independent spatial measurements. The earlier-cutoff sensitivity probes timing dependence. Measurement at nominal reception may already partly reflect contact. Event/tracking disagreement can exclude otherwise valid actions.
* **Roster versus active coverage.** The pose release carries roster rows and interpolated XY for inactive players. All-roster coverage is not on-pitch coverage. Active intervals use supplied substitution timings and can themselves be imperfect. Do not derive opponent pressure from unfiltered pose roster positions.
* **Narrow target.** A successful forward pass within 2 s and gaining 5 m is one measurable outcome. It treats shots and valuable longer carries as target negatives. Labels are not an expert assessment of the quality of a reception. A zero-duration event can reflect a one-touch action at 100 ms resolution, not an instantaneous biological response.
* **Retention and EPV are limited.** The supplied pass/foul/loss labels support only an explicit retention proxy; shots and clearances remain unknown for that proxy. Vendor EPV is treated descriptively. Its model/training details are not independently audited, and it may share dependencies with other vendor outputs. EPV change is not a measured scoring outcome.
* **Simple models.** Fixed L2 logistic models can miss nonlinear pressure effects and interactions. With little data, richer models would bring substantial tuning and overfitting risk. An orientation feature might partly stand in for context omitted from the baseline. No algorithm search is justified here.
* **Conditional uncertainty.** The paired five-minute block bootstrap holds fitted models fixed and describes one test match. It does not estimate uncertainty over new matches or propagate pose/label error. Adjacent blocks and possessions can remain dependent. Category Wilson intervals ignore clustering and are explicitly descriptive.
* **Sensitivity is diagnostic.** Configurations were specified before orientation fitting. Different gating choices change the sample. Inspect all results rather than selecting a favourable threshold. Angular perturbations are illustrative stress tests, not a physical uncertainty model. The midfield subset is small and exploratory.
* **Reproducibility versus external validation.** Checksums, code, tests and scripts reproduce this calculation. They do not validate the provider's identities, pose, pressure, EPV or event labels. The publisher reports roughly 97% tracking identity accuracy; this project does not independently confirm that figure.

A stronger next study needs more independent matches and competitions, manually checked reception/contact timing and headings on a stratified sample, richer pre-reception context, and an external evaluation set never used for feature development. A causal coaching recommendation would require a different design.

The pipeline's “causal” timing refers to the timestamps consumed by feature construction. The vendor's pose and tracking estimators may use temporal context or smoothing internally; their complete inference/training pipelines are not supplied. This project cannot certify that the provider-generated estimates would have been available online at reception, or that the source estimators never used later video. The claim is an offline, timestamp-bounded ablation.
