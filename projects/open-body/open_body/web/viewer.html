Read the measurement and evaluation limits

The primary target is a successful pass that gains at least 5 m within 2 s of reception. A good carry, shot or slower pass can be a target negative. The baseline uses location, opponent proximity and pre-reception speed. Added inputs describe shoulder heading relative to attack, ball and nearest opponent, plus earlier turning.

Only two matches and four teams are available. Visibility selection, pose/identity error and omitted context remain. Neither model establishes a causal benefit of body orientation. Receptions are measured at 10 Hz event precision; 25 Hz pose does not provide verified first-touch timing. Midfield findings do not consistently favour orientation.

The source release has a long tail of pose/native-XY mismatches. Analysis samples require shoulder-width/error gates, causal circular smoothing and native-tracking agreement. The animation displays a skeleton even if it fails an analytic gate, with its status labelled. Full audit, configuration and per-event predictions are in the repository.
